# Fastener reach check (CDR-01 / CDR-24)

Generated by `python analysis/fastener_reach.py` from `meshes.json` (scene list, world mm). Do not edit by hand.

Criterion: engagement >= 1.5 d in PLA, >= 1.0 d in steel / Al / brass. Engagement is the axial overlap of the shank (tip to head bearing face) with the receiving thread: T-nut, heat-set insert or tapped horn by axial extent, PLA pilot by point-in-mesh ring test at r = d/2 - 0.1 mm. 'Avail.' is the threaded length the receiving body offers on this axis (for PLA: material inside d/2 along the 15 mm search window, i.e. pilot plus any solid beyond it); 'gap' is how far short of it the tip stops.

**The CAD still carries the pre-CDR fastener lengths.** FAILs below are the as-modelled state and are expected; the CDR disposition column says what the ruling changes. Re-run after the CAD occurrences are updated.
