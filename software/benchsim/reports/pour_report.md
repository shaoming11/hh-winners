# Phase 6: analytic liquid model and closed-loop pouring

Generated by `python -m benchsim.pour_trials` (simulation only). **pour trials: 10/10 within +-2 g (mean -0.28 g, sd 0.06 g, max |e| 0.38 g, 17.1 s mean per pour); spill shown: True; PASS**

## What is modelled (analytic, not CFD)

- **Tilted cylinder** (`chem/liquid.py: TiltedCylinder`): beaker inside r 26.8 mm, h 72.8 mm; flat horizontal free surface (no slosh, no meniscus). Volume vs tilt and surface height above the lip by quadrature; V_c(theta) and its inverse theta_first(V). Checked against the closed forms (pi r^2 (h - r tan theta) for small tilt, 2/3 r^2 h for the ungula) in `tests/test_pour.py`.
- **Weir flow over the lip** (approximation): Q = Cd 2/3 sqrt(2g) w (h - h_hold)^1.5 with Cd 0.62, effective lip width 14 mm, hold-up head 0.5 mm (all assumed, `[liquid]`). Viscosity and surface tension beyond h_hold are ignored.
- **Stream**: 30 ms lip run-down, then a drag-free ballistic fall from the outer lip at the critical-depth velocity sqrt(g 2h/3) along the pour direction. Mass in flight is tracked as parcels; a parcel is caught by the highest opening it falls through (beaker: inner radius; funnel: rim radius 50 mm -> flask after 0.8 s) in proportion to the overlap of a 3 mm jet disc with the opening; the rest is `Ledger.spill` (removed from the balance, even if it would land on the pan).
- **Impact force** of the landing stream (m_dot v_z) is applied to the receiver beaker (`impact_force = true`), so the scale reads the stream overshoot; **after-drip** 0.10 mL per pour (tau 0.5 s); **residual film** 0.4 mL never drains.

The few mg spilled per trial are after-drip that falls from the lip while the harness untilts the beaker back to the pre-pour pose after DONE (spilled during the pour itself: max 0.0024 g). **Cut law.** Configured a = 0.30 g, b = 0.15 s predicts 0.46 g after the cut on average; the scale actually gained 0.15 g after the cut. ## Flow tracking (architecture 5.3 / 9.1)

Acceptance in the architecture: q* tracked within +-1 g/s after 1.5 s for 1-8 g/s. Tested on the MuJoCo-free synthetic plant (same lip-flow relations, `SyntheticPour`) with a constant q*, over 1/2/4/8 g/s, fills 55/80 mL, lip width 10/14/20 mm and hold-up head 0/0.5/1 mm (72 cases); error = max |q_hat - q*| from 1.5 s to 6 s after first drop:

| Kp deg/(g/s) | Ki deg/s/(g/s) | cases within 1 g/s | median worst | worst |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.15 | 0.30 | 37 / 72 | 0.86 | 4.57 |
| 0.15 | 1.50 | 72 / 72 | 0.23 | 0.88 |

The architecture's initial Ki = 0.3 misses the criterion: with the feed-forward holding the current flow, the PI acts as an integral flow loop with time constant (tau_lip + G Kp)/(G Ki), where G = rho |dV_c/dtheta| is about 3 g/deg and tau_lip = 1.3-2.7 s is the drain time of the liquid standing above the lip. That gives 2-3.5 s with Ki 0.3. The config uses Ki = 1.50 (Kp unchanged, 0.15). Retune on hardware. In the MuJoCo pours, |q_hat - q*| includes the set-point ramp-down near the target (q* falls at 0.6 q per second), so it is larger than in the constant set-point test: worst per trial 1.93, 1.96, 1.87, 1.97, 1.88, 2.09, 1.98, 2.10, 1.80, 1.93 g/s.

P1 sits in the WS-02 locating ring (2.2 mm radial play), so the receiver offset is applied to the whole scale pan with P1 in it, as if WS-01 were clamped off its JG-06 jig position. The arm pours along the unchanged native plan. Fill 70 mL, seed 31.

| spill B without the spill check | (-30.7, 5.2) | PAUSE_LOW_REAGENT | 28.68 | 43.70 | 0.42 | 105.0 | 17.9 | manifold end 105 deg reached at 28.7 g |

- **A (total miss):** nothing reaches P1, so first drop is never detected; the ramp stops at theta_table(46 mL - offset) and the controller untilts and pauses (`PAUSE_NO_FLOW`, "refill, or the stream misses the receiver"). Everything that crossed the lip before the untilt is spilled.
- **B (partial miss):** part of the stream lands in P1, so the loop runs. The flow the scale sees is low, so the controller tilts further. The mass-deficit check (an addition to section 5: tilt-implied mass out vs scale gain, armed 2.0 s after first drop, 1.0 s hold) pauses with `PAUSE_SPILL` and leaves the rest in the source. Without it the pour runs to the 105 deg manifold end (`PAUSE_LOW_REAGENT`) and empties the source. The check fires late because the liquid standing above the lip (6-18 g at 1-8 g/s) looks like a deficit during the start-up transient. Its smallest margin in the 10 normal trials was 5.63 g (negative would mean a false alarm). The real fix is upstream: check the receiver position (JG-06, the place check) before pouring.
- **Toward the pourer is a collision, not a spill:** during the tilt-in the source beaker's lower edge swings below the receiver rim. With the receiver moved 33 mm toward the pourer, the developer beaker's floor strikes P1's wall at theta 34 deg (MuJoCo contacts beaker_dev_floor / beaker_P1 wall boxes; the scale read 27 g at contact). Seen while choosing the spill geometry, not part of these runs.

The ramp lag is kinematic, not the liquid load: the same lag appears with an empty beaker. Between theta about 85 and 96 deg the native manifold (phase-3 continuation IK, which allows at most 17 deg per step) moves elbow and wrist flex by up to 17 deg per degree of tilt. At 10 deg/s that saturates both actuators (load +-1000 per-mille), so the beaker trails the command until 105 deg. The liquid has mostly left by then, so the delivered mass is unaffected. A planner-side fix (a smoother branch, or a joint-speed-aware tilt rate on that stretch) belongs to phase 3.

## Setup and harness notes

- Receiver: P1 teleported to scale_pan at (620.0, 407.0, 63.8) mm. Developer: dev filled with 48.0 mL developer (49.92 g) in the Ledger.
- The pourer runs the first 6 segments of the native step-2 plan (grip height 44 mm from reports/native_plans.json) through the phase-5 Executor; the grasp is classified from the jaw angle (grasped 8.26 deg vs 7.72) and held by the phase-5 weld. Pre-pour reached at 11.1 s sim.
- Tilt actuator: theta -> joint goal by linear interpolation of the plan's tilt-in (theta from forward kinematics of the held beaker) and the 1-deg pour manifold; goals go to SimArm.set_goal at 40 Hz. Largest commanded - actual beaker tilt while pouring: 0.38 deg.
- Scale samples come from MegaClient (STREAM, TARE) on the simulated HX711 (80 Hz, 0.07 g noise). The flexure reading includes the stream impact. Total wall time 35 s (5 processes).
- Traces (`reports/pour_traces/*.csv`, every 2nd control tick): t, state, theta_cmd, theta_beaker (actual), m_f, q_hat, q_set, scale_w (latest raw sample), m_true (Ledger), q_lip (true lip flow g/s), in_flight, spilled, src_g.

## Known gaps

- Every `[liquid]` coefficient is assumed; the plant time constant (liquid above the lip) scales with lip width and hold-up head. The gain choice was checked over lip width 10-20 mm and hold-up head 0-1 mm on the synthetic plant only.
- No slosh, no wave over the lip on fast untilt, no teapot effect (liquid running back under the lip at low flow), no viscosity: the architecture's two-stage untilt exists for slosh this model cannot produce.
- Spilled liquid leaves the balance even if it would land on the WS-02 pan and be weighed.
- The fill offset and the cut law are simulator values. Both must be re-measured on the bench (architecture 5.2, 5.4).
- Settled is |q_hat| < 0.25 g/s and window sigma < 0.15 g: the architecture's 0.05 g is below the per-sample HX711 noise.
- Water pours (step 7) use the same controller; `[pour.water] fill_offset_ml` is assumed equal to the developer value and was not simulated here.
