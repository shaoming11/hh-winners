## (a) Yeast dosing

Model: per-revolution g/rev drawn from N(0.850, (0.06 x 0.850)^2) with a seeded RNG. Yeast leaves only on new screw travel, so the 1/4-turn reverse after each AUG costs 1/4 rev of dispensing at the start of the next AUG. Host: first pass 85 % of target by revolutions, scale verification, at most 2 top-ups of round((target - m)/g_per_rev, 0.1). P2 is teleported between its pick pocket and the scale pan (the transfer-arm move is the sequencer's job); its empty tare on the simulated HX711 was 18.017 g.

Landing: outlet 0.391 mm from the P2 axis at the auger station (limit 21.800 = inner radius - outlet radius), 33.9 mm above the lip; receiver = P2.

Calibration (architecture 6, 3 x 10 rev into the tared cup): **0.8143 g/rev** (model mean 0.850 per dispensing revolution). Runs: 0.842 g/rev, 0.808 g/rev, 0.792 g/rev.

Where the negative bias comes from: every AUG starts by re-covering the previous 0.25-turn anti-dribble reverse, and in this model that travel dispenses nothing ([auger_model] recover_dispenses = false, assumed). The documented top-up formula is used unchanged. If the real screw behaves this way, adding reverse_rev to each commanded pass would remove most of the bias.

## Approximations

- Auger: grams per revolution are i.i.d. normal per revolution of new screw travel; no bridging, no compaction, no dependence on hopper level until the hopper is empty. The fall from the outlet is instant.
- The ledger applies the yeast weight to P2 as an external force, and the simulated scale reads it through the HX711 model (noise 0.07 g at 80 Hz, mean of 16 samples).
- Foam: first-order kinetics after a fixed induction delay. The headspace ignores the liquid carried into the foam. The column is a cylinder of neck-bore diameter drawn through the funnel, which is ignored. Collapse is a single exponential; overflow above the standing height is only bookkept. None of this is CFD.
- Stage and carousel use the MuJoCo position actuators driven by the simulated Mega smoothstep. The closed slider-crank uses a soft connect equality, so linkage errors are constraint compliance.
